3.	Explain encapsulation with a real-world example (such as a MobilePhone hiding its internals). Discuss JavaBeans and their connection to encapsulation, and explain why immutability is a special form of encapsulation, including its advantages in multi-threaded applications.

4.	Differentiate between an abstract class and an interface with a real-world analogy. Explain the role of default and static methods in interfaces (Java 8+) and their impact on backward compatibility.

5.	Why does Java not support multiple inheritance through classes? Explain the Diamond Problem with an example and show how interfaces resolve it.

6.	Explain the Thread lifecycle (New, Runnable, Running, Blocked/Waiting, Terminated). Compare the two ways of creating threads: extending the Thread class vs implementing the Runnable interface.

7.	Differentiate between sleep(), wait(), notify(), and notifyAll(). Explain how they are used for inter-thread communication.

8.Define race condition and deadlock with real-world examples. Explain the synchronized keyword (methods and blocks), the volatile keyword (when it is and is not sufficient), and how java.util.concurrent classes such as AtomicInteger and ConcurrentHashMap help.
